# Product Vision

Music Cataloguing App gives physical records a trustworthy digital second life. The primary experience is identify → compare → confirm → save → rediscover.

- Tagline: **Old Records. New Discoveries.**
- Optimize first for vinyl collectors, DJs, inherited collections, and curious beginners.
- Minimize effort without hiding edition uncertainty.
- Favor an honest partial identification over a confident wrong pressing.
- Preserve original user evidence, provider provenance, and edition details.
- Keep the working individual flow ahead of batch, AI discovery, valuation, and decorative analytics.
- Demo fixtures must be fictional, clearly labeled, and distinguishable from live provider results.
- Never describe unfinished, unavailable, or simulated integrations as functional.
